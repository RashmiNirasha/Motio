How to label
  • Pincer: the object is held mainly between the pads or tips of the thumb and index finger (neat or inferior pincer).
  • Other grasp: palmar, raking, whole-hand or multi-finger grasp; object enclosed by the palm or several fingers.
  • No grasp: the hand is touching or near the object but not holding it.
  • Unclear: thumb or index hidden, blur, or you are unsure. Unclear clips are not used for training, so use this freely.

All clips are children (ChildPlay's own child/adult annotation). ChildPlay has no ages, so confirm the age once per video from the whole-scene picture: it applies to every clip from that video. The title-based hint is only a suggestion. Videos marked 5 or older are hidden by default and left out by train_pincer.py --under-5-only.

Keys: 1–4 label and go to next, Y/O/N video age, ←/→ previous/next, Space pause, ,/. step one frame when paused.

Clips are only used for training where MediaPipe also found the hand in at least 20 consecutive frames, because the live app runs on MediaPipe landmarks.
